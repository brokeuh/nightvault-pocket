# Nightvault – ideas for later

Things we talked about and parked. Newest at the top.

## App look
- **Icon that follows the phone's colours** – add a one-colour ("monochrome") version of the app icon, so Android 13+ phones with *Themed icons* switched on can recolour it to the wallpaper. Costs nothing if a phone ignores it. (Changing the icon per app theme is not practical: Android only refreshes an installed app's icon every few days.)
- **New logo in the top bar** – replace the old moon icon in the header of Pocket and Nightvault (PC) with the fanned-cards logo.

## Proxies and Photoshop
- **Test the Proxyshop offline plugin on the home PC** – link this chat to the PC, then make test cards (creature, instant, land, multicolour) with Trix Minimalist, also once with the internet off.
- **Adapt more fan-made templates** to Proxyshop's auto-fill and auto text size (send the PSDs; reference boxes get drawn by hand where missing).
- **Proxy printer with our own design** – one Nightvault layout for every game, 9 cards on an A4 sheet with cut lines, straight from a deck.
- **"Copy card text" button** – to paste a card's name, cost, type and text into other tools.
- **Export card data** – a deck, set or the whole collection as CSV/JSON (also readable by Photoshop's Data Sets).
